Bhakti Tradition Map

Interactive spatial–temporal visualisation of Bhakti saint networks · Under Dr. Priyadarshi Patnaik, Dept. of Humanities & Social Sciences, IIT Kharagpur · Nov 2025

The Bhakti movement spans roughly 600–1900 CE and five major traditions — Vaishnava, Shaiva, Sant, Sufi and Shakta. The scholarship on it is excellent but scattered: static maps, linear chronologies, and biographical records split across a dozen languages. You can read about Kabir, or about the Alvars, but you can't easily see how devotional traditions moved across the subcontinent, or which lineages clustered where and when.

I built a web platform that makes that question answerable interactively — a three-tier system where a researcher can filter 1,300 years of saint records by tradition, century, gender, language or philosophical school and watch the map redraw in under 250ms.

Engineering decisions

  • Vanilla JS over React. No build step, smaller bundle, and direct DOM control for Leaflet marker manipulation — chosen deliberately so a future student maintainer could pick it up without toolchain overhead.
  • MongoDB over a relational store. Saint records are radically uneven — some have precise dates and samadhi coordinates, most don't. A document schema with optional fields modelled that honestly instead of forcing nulls into rigid columns.
  • Marker clustering with chunked loading. Rendering hundreds of discrete points degraded badly; clustering at a 50px radius with progressive de-clustering at zoom 15 kept interaction smooth.
  • Server-side geocoding via Nominatim. Contributors submit place names, not coordinates. The API resolves them on submission, with caching and batching to respect rate limits.

Hardest problem

Historical uncertainty. Many saints have disputed or unknown dates, and the same place appears under three spellings across sources. Rather than claiming a precision the sources don't support, I modelled period ranges instead of exact dates and leaned on Nominatim's variant handling for place names — a data-modelling decision, not a coding one.

Architecture

  • Frontend — ES6+ modules, Leaflet 1.9.4, markercluster 1.5.3, CartoDB tiles with light/dark/auto theming.
  • Backend — Node 18 + Express 4.19, MongoDB driver 6.18, CORS-restricted origins, server-side input validation.
  • Data — MongoDB Atlas, indexed on tradition, type, gender, language and startYear, plus a text index for search.
  • Deploy — Vercel (static frontend) and Render (API), with environment-based endpoint switching.

Also built in

  • Community contribution flow with place autocomplete and validation.
  • Debounced live search (300ms) across names, traditions and philosophy.
  • aria-live screen-reader announcements and a mobile-first responsive layout.
